Vampires in New York

New York Harbor, 1918

James Marston comes home from the war and sets himself up as a private detective. His first case takes him from a hotel room in Manhattan to a race against time in the halls of Columbia University.

Two voices

Every panel is told twice: by a narrator who sees everything, and by Marston, who only sees what he can bear to. Show one, the other, or both.

Panels that move

Ships roll on the harbor, the market crowds shift, and each scene carries its own sound. Slow it down, speed it up, or turn it off.
